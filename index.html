Privacy Policy

Aeralume: Endless Flight
Effective: 16 September 2026

This privacy policy explains how Azhar Bhatti (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you use Aeralume: Endless Flight (“Aeralume” or “the game”).

Information handled by the game

Aeralume stores your personal best score locally on your device. This local score is not sent to a server operated by us. It remains on the device until you clear the game’s data or uninstall the game.

If you choose to use the optional worldwide leaderboard, Aeralume uses Google Play Games Services. Through that service, the game may access or display your Google Play Games gamer identity, including your player identifier, gamer display name, profile information, leaderboard rank and score. Your score is submitted to Google Play Games so it can appear on the worldwide leaderboard.

How information is used

  • To authenticate you with Google Play Games when you request online leaderboard features.
  • To submit, retrieve and display leaderboard scores and rankings.
  • To remember your best score locally on your device.

Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell personal information. Online leaderboard information is processed by Google through Google Play Games Services. Google may process account, device and service-usage information under the Google Privacy Policy and the controls provided in your Google Play Games profile.

Advertising, analytics and purchases

The current version of Aeralume does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, in-app purchases or a developer-operated user-account system.

Device permissions

Aeralume does not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Data security

Leaderboard communication is handled through Google Play Games Services using platform security protections. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we limit information handling to what is needed for the game features described above.

Retention and deletion

Your local best score remains on your device until you clear the game’s storage or uninstall it. Google Play Games leaderboard and profile information is retained and managed by Google according to its policies and your Google Play Games settings. You can manage or delete Play Games data through your Google account and Play Games settings. You may also contact us about a privacy or deletion request.

Children’s privacy

Aeralume is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated service. Google Play Games availability and account handling are governed by Google’s applicable age and account requirements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.
