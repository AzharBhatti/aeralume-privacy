Delete My Data

Aeralume: Endless Flight does not operate its own account system or server. The only data associated with you is:

  • Your Google Play Games player ID and profile name (used for the worldwide leaderboard)
  • Scores you've submitted to the leaderboard

How to delete this data

This data is managed by Google Play Games, not by Aeralume directly. You can delete it yourself at any time:

  1. Open Google Account → Data & Privacy and remove Play Games activity, or
  2. Open the Play Games app → your profile → Data & Privacy settings, and delete your Play Games data

Your local best score is stored only on your device and is removed automatically when you uninstall the app.
